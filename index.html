Clipboard Manager — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Clipboard Manager does not collect, send or sell your data. Your whole clipboard history is stored locally in your browser, on your own computer. No account, no server of ours, no sync.

Data the extension stores

DataWhereWhy
Text you copy in the browserchrome.storage.local (your device)To show your history again
Folder names, favoriteschrome.storage.localTo organize items
Settings (theme, auto-delete days…)chrome.storage.localTo remember your choices
Pro license key (if any)chrome.storage.localTo unlock Pro features

Only text is stored. Images and files you copy are never recorded. Each item is limited to 20,000 characters.

The only network connection

The free version makes no network connections at all. If you buy Pro, the extension contacts api.lemonsqueezy.com to activate your key, and about once a day to check that your subscription is still active. That request contains your license key and a device label such as "Clipboard Manager on Windows". It never includes any clipboard content.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • storage, unlimitedStorage — store your history on your device. History can reach 10,000 items, which exceeds the default 5 MB quota.
  • clipboardWrite — copy an item again when you click it.
  • clipboardRead — used only when you press the "Get current clipboard" button. The clipboard is never read in the background or on a schedule.
  • alarms — one hourly alarm that deletes old items according to your auto-delete setting, and re-checks a Pro subscription.
  • downloads — save a file when you press Export.
  • contextMenus — add items to the right-click menu.
  • Access to all websites — a copy can happen on any page, so the content script must be present on every page to notice it and to paste back into the page you are on. It does not read page content and sends nothing anywhere.

You control your data

  • Pause capturing in Settings. We recommend doing this before copying passwords.
  • Delete single items with ✕, or everything with "Clear all".
  • Export everything to CSV, text, Markdown or JSON at any time.
  • Uninstall and Chrome deletes all of the extension's data with it.

Cursor Studio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Cursor Studio does not collect, store or sell any of your personal data. It has no server of its own, no analytics, no ads and no tracking pixels.

What the extension stores

WhereWhat
chrome.storage.syncYour preferences: cursor, size, smoothing, opacity, trail, click effect, sound pack, volume, and websites where you switched it off. Chrome syncs these between your own signed-in devices. We cannot read any of it.
chrome.storage.localLicense state: the license key, the device ID issued by Lemon Squeezy, and when the license was last checked.

What the extension never touches

  • Your browsing history or the URLs of pages you open.
  • Page content, text you type, passwords, forms or cookies.
  • Mouse coordinates are used only momentarily to draw the cursor — never stored, never transmitted.
  • Contacts, location, camera, microphone or files on your computer.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Access to all websites — lets the extension draw its cursor layer over the page you are viewing. It only reads mouse coordinates and sends nothing anywhere.
  • storage — remembers your cursor and settings.
  • activeTab — used only by the popup to offer "Turn off on this site". Only the hostname is kept, locally.
  • alarms — re-checks a Pro license every 12 hours. Does nothing for free users.

The only network connection

The extension contacts api.lemonsqueezy.com only when you press Activate on a license, or to re-check a Pro license every 12 hours. It sends the license key and device ID and receives whether the license is still valid. Free users never trigger any network connection.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the extension erases everything above. To free a license slot first, open the extension → Pro tab → Remove license from this device.

We do not collect, sell or share your browsing history or personal data. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not run our own servers.

You can delete all of it at any time by removing the extension or clearing its data in Chrome.

Data sent to Lemon Squeezy

When you activate, re-check or deactivate a Pro license, the extension sends your license key and an activation ID to api.lemonsqueezy.com. It repeats this check about twice a day so expired subscriptions are detected. We never see your card details.

Website content the extension reads

On supported marketplace product pages, the extension reads the page you are viewing (title, price, rating, specifications, image and video links) only to perform the action you request: copying a link, inspecting images, downloading media, or adding the product to your comparison list. Media files are fetched directly from the marketplace's own servers. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party.
