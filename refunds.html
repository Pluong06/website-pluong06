Refund Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

  • 14-day money-back guarantee on every Pro purchase, no questions asked.
  • Every product has a free plan, so you can try it before you pay.
  • Refunds are processed by Paddle, our reseller and Merchant of Record.

This policy applies to Pro purchases of Clipboard Manager, Cursor Studio and E-Com & Affiliate Assistant.

1. 14-day money-back guarantee

If you are not happy with a Pro purchase, you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of the payment date. This applies to the first payment of a weekly, monthly or yearly subscription, to each renewal payment, and to lifetime licenses.

2. How to request a refund

Email luongshinichi@gmail.com with the email address you used at checkout and your order number, which is in your Paddle receipt. You can also contact Paddle directly at paddle.net.

3. How refunds are paid

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com, the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Approved refunds are returned by Paddle to your original payment method. Depending on your bank, it may take 5–10 business days to appear.

4. After a refund

When a payment is refunded, the related license is deactivated and Pro features are switched off. The free plan keeps working, and the data stored in the extension is not deleted.

5. Cancelling a subscription

Cancelling stops future renewals. It does not by itself refund the current period. If you want a refund for a payment made in the last 14 days, ask for it as described above.

6. Your legal rights

This policy does not affect any rights you have under consumer protection laws in your country.
